## What Is It?

[Easytools](http://easy.tools) is a set of tools for creators and business owners who sell digital products such as courses, subscriptions, memberships, or licenses. At its core, there is a highly optimized checkout process surrounded by automated workflows and tools needed to deliver products and work with clients. All of this is extremely simple and well designed, allowing most of the selling process to be set up in just 10 minutes.


## Story

My friend and I created Easytools primarily for ourselves, as our online presence created a need to sell and deliver various products, and we weren't happy with the experience offered by the tools available on the market. A few months later, our clients started asking whether they could get similar tools for their businesses, and this is how our side project became our core product.

## Tools

The following tools are available on the platform and connected to each other. For example, collected testimonials can be automatically displayed in the checkout for the product they're assigned to.

- **Store**: go-to solution for handling online payments on top of Stripe.
- **Websites**: AI website builder that creates pages matched to your products.
- **Emails**: create email campaigns in minutes, send them to your audience, and see exactly how much each one made you, all directly from Easytools.
- **Courses**: convenient player experience for delivering online courses, PDFs, and files instantly after purchase.
- **Testimonials**: collect custom reviews and video testimonials, customize them, prepare them for social media, and embed them wherever you need, including checkout pages to increase conversions.

## Key Features

- **1-click checkout**: customer details are saved across all Easytools merchants, allowing customers to buy with a single click, which drastically improves conversion rates.
- **Checkout recovery**: automated emails take care of abandoned carts and failed subscription payments.
- **Customer portal**: clients have access to all their products within a single login, allowing them to manage subscriptions, purchases, and refund requests.
- **Integrations**: Discord, WordPress, CRMs, transactional email platforms such as Mailchimp, and automation platforms such as Make or Zapier.
