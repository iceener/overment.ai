## What Is It?

[Eduweb](https://eduweb.pl) is an educational platform with video courses focused on design and tech, delivered over 15 years to more than 200,000 clients.

## Story

Education lies at my core and is an important part of my life mission. Along the road I joined Eduweb, which turned that calling into a business and became the starting point for pretty much all of my other initiatives.

## What's Next

In 2026 Eduweb is going through important changes because of how AI affected the way we learn and the way educational content can be created. The old model, where a single course was crafted over months of hard work and shipped as video material, is no longer valid.

I'm actively working on the next chapter, which translates into new workflows, tools, and a rethought learning experience.

More to come.
